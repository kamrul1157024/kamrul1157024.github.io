Full fine-tuning updates every W[i,j] with ∂L/∂W = Xᵀ·G — the gradient of weight (i,j) is the dot product of column i of X with column j of G. LoRA freezes W and learns A (N×r) and B (r×M) instead: the same full gradient XᵀG exists implicitly, but only its low-rank projections ∂L/∂A = (XᵀG)·Bᵀ and ∂L/∂B = Aᵀ·(XᵀG) are ever applied.
